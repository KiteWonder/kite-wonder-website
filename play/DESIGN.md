# Number Search design standard

| Levels | Grid | Digits | Targets | Directions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 Easy | 8x8 | 4 | 6 | Right, down |
| 3–4 Medium | 10x10 | 5 | 8 | Right, down, down-right, down-left |
| 5–6 Hard | 12x12 | 6 | 10 | All eight |

Every target must occur exactly once across all eight directions. Show zeros. Tap endpoints. Instructions must reflect difficulty. Verify all boards before publication.
